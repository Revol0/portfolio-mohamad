About

A designer who understands both design and production.

For an in-house role, I focus on building a coherent visual language for the company rather than producing isolated graphics.

I am a graphic designer based in Madinah, Saudi Arabia. My practical experience includes graphic design and print production, preparing artwork for output, working with printing equipment, plotter cutting and acrylic laser cutting, and adapting visual materials across different formats.

I have also developed front-end and web design skills. That technical background helps me think about visual identity across both physical and digital touchpoints and communicate more effectively with developers.
